Sound effects were processed with `tools/make_sfx.py` (commands in `tools/make_sfx.sh`). Music was cut into loops with
`tools/make_music.py` (commands and loop points in `tools/make_music.sh`).

Every file under `assets/` has a row above. Only CC0 and CC-BY assets are used (no share-alike, no non-commercial),
plus the Godot Engine under MIT. The CC-BY crowd sounds (crowd, crowd_loud) and the New York, Memphis and Seattle
music need the credit above (title, author, license with its link, source and changes) to stay with the game: the
in-game CREW & CREDITS screen shows the same items (`CREDITS_LIST` in `scripts/title.gd`, the license links from its
`LICENSE_URLS`), followed by the Godot license text. Keep the two in step when an asset changes.

Not part of the game: the preview images in `docs/art/` (made by the same scripts), the raw How to Play captures in
`tools/art/howto_raw/` (the game's own screenshots; `tools/` is not exported), the release cover and screenshots in
`docs/release/` (the game's own art and screenshots, for the itch.io page), and the 30-second music samples in
`docs/audio_preview/`, a listening list for the owner (sources and licenses in `docs/audio_preview/LIST.md`; the
picked tracks have rows above).
