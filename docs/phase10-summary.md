# Phase 10 summary: Legends, Chapter 2 (the Fathers)

Version stays 1.0.0. Published to the web link from `a8d2f52` (plus this summary). Release files rebuilt in
`build/release/` (not in git): the web ZIP and the debug APK (the signed APK needs the owner's key,
`docs/release/ANDROID.md`).

## What is new
- Chapter 2 of LEGENDS, open at 36 stars or 12 legend tokens (whichever comes first): six new original legends
  (Rooftop, Ankles, Midrange, Freight, Eyes Closed, Lockbox), each with a challenge game, tokens 1/2/3 by level (21
  more, 42 in all), a finale to 21 (Rooftop, Midrange and Freight), story scenes and portraits.
- The NIGHT PARK court (a blacktop under light poles), the "Headspin" era track (CC0) and ISO BALL rules: crossovers
  beat the man in front a bit more often, dunks start a little further out.
- A new challenge type, SCORE: Lockbox asks you to score 6 of your team's points yourself and win (to 11), the
  hardest challenge on purpose.
- Six new signature moves: Rooftop's cradle dunk, Ankles' double crossover, Midrange's fadeaway (a new animation),
  Freight's shoulder, Eyes Closed's no-look pass, Lockbox's chase-down block.
- Six new mentor perks, sized after the ramp check to about +2 points (rise up, handle, pull up, strong hold, blind
  pass, quick hands).
- The Hall of Legends and the Road Trip draft show both eras (a Fathers row and a Grandfathers row); still only one
  legend may start, whatever his era, next to one boss.
- Owner calls during the phase: Lockbox kept at 6 points; the Chapter 1 finale made a bit harder (its legends rated
  55 + 20, was 52); Eyes Closed's comeback starts from 0-1.
