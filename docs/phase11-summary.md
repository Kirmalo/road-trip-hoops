# Phase 11 summary: Legends, Chapter 3 (the Present)

Version stays 1.0.0. Published to the web link from this commit. Release files rebuilt in `build/release/` (not in
git): the web ZIP and the debug APK (the signed APK needs the owner's key, `docs/release/ANDROID.md`).

## What is new
- Chapter 3 of LEGENDS, open at 48 stars or 30 legend tokens (whichever comes first): six new original legends, each
  with a challenge game, story scenes and portraits. Tokens 1/2/3 by level (21 more, 63 in all). A finale to 21
  (Deep End, Toolbox and Skylight).
  - Deep End (Calloway Whitlock): a fire challenge, signature step-back jumper (a new animation).
  - Toolbox (Theo Lindqvist): a comeback from 0-1, signature coast-to-coast.
  - Stretch (Sigurd Halvorsen): a race to 11 before 10, signature pick-and-pop.
  - Switch (Ellery Achterberg): score 5 yourself (to 11), the hardest one on purpose, signature steal-and-go.
  - Skylight (Bodie Castellano): 2 stops, signature lob catcher.
  - Zigzag (Bastien Moreau): hold him under 6, signature Euro-step (a new animation).
- The BIG STAGE court (teal and coral), the "Techno drive" track (Centurion_of_war, CC-BY 4.0) and the SPACE THE FLOOR
  rules: threes fall a little more, wider spacing, pull-ups right after a crossover.
- Six new mentor perks, sized after the ramp check to about +2 stat points each: Deep End's off the dribble, Toolbox's
  extra pass, Stretch's catch and shoot, Switch's contest, Skylight's board work, Zigzag's side step.
- Era teams in Quick Game: the Grandfathers, the Fathers and the Present (each era's finale three, stats evened to 50,
  signature moves on, no perks). A team opens once its three legends are unlocked. The era courts (OLD GYM, NIGHT PARK,
  BIG STAGE) join the COURT list with their rules and music.
- The Hall of Legends, the Road Trip draft and the timeline show three eras. Still only one legend may start, whatever
  his era, next to one boss.
- Owner calls during the phase: the names (Sigurd Halvorsen, Bastien for Zigzag), the looks and both new moves, teal and
  coral, the court name BIG STAGE, the spacing kept at 2 ft, Chapter 3's difficulty accepted as measured, a darker teal
  for the Present's tags (#0a7385), the era team balance left as is, and the six perk sizes.
