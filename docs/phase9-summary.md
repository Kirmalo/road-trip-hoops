# Phase 9 summary: Legends, Chapter 1 (the Grandfathers)

Version stays 1.0.0. Published to the web link from `000196b`. Release files rebuilt in `build/release/` (not in git):
the web ZIP and the debug APK (the signed APK needs the owner's key, `docs/release/ANDROID.md`).

## What is new
- LEGENDS on the title, open at 24 stars: the era tour, Chapter 1 with six original legends (Steeple, Gate, Set Shot,
  Boxcar, Velvet, Quarterback), each with a challenge game (tuned goals in `docs/qa/phase9-challenges.md`), tokens
  1/2/3 by level (21 in all), a finale to 21, story scenes, the OLD GYM court, the Bebop era track and OLD SCHOOL rules.
- Played with your current Road Trip starters (the Kings without a trip); a challenge never touches the trip.
- Hall of Legends (CREW & CREDITS and the tour): plaques, details, REPLAY STORY, mentors (one small perk per player,
  regular players only, Road Trip games only).
- Legends in the Road Trip draft: one legend may start next to one boss; boss-level at the stage reached.
- Signature moves, including Steeple's new hook-shot animation.
- Owner requests during the phase: a level picker for replays; offensive rebounds cut from 56% to about 26% of misses
  (box-outs, one crasher, a light box-out assist for you), game length kept at about 9.5 min.
